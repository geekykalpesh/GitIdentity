# Privacy Policy for GitIdentity

**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026

**GitIdentity** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GitIdentity operates regarding data collection, usage, and storage.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
GitIdentity is a **100% offline, local desktop application** designed to help developers manage multiple GitHub SSH identities and repository configurations locally. 

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, names, email addresses, or usage statistics.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** GitIdentity contains zero telemetry, tracking scripts, or analytics services.
- **No Cloud Servers:** All your SSH keys, identity profiles, and repository settings remain strictly on your local machine (`~/.ssh` and local `.gitconfig` files).

---

### 2. Local File Access & Storage
GitIdentity reads and writes configuration files stored locally on your device only when explicitly requested by you:
- `~/.ssh/config` and local SSH key files (for switching GitHub SSH identities).
- Local `.gitconfig` files and repository-specific `.git/config` files (to update `user.name` and `user.email`).

All file operations take place locally on your computer and are never transmitted over the internet or to third-party servers.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
GitIdentity interacts with Git services (such as GitHub) locally using your system's installed Git and SSH clients according to your local configuration. GitIdentity does not transmit any credentials or SSH key pairs to external third parties.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
GitIdentity does not address or collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
